That is the whole studio in one sentence. A human with ideas. Claude, by Anthropic, with the hands.

The founder does not write code. Not one line. The founder is disabled and lives with ADHD, autism and vision problems. Those limits are real.

So the lab is built around them. Nothing here depends on holding a hundred things in your head at once.
