Mission briefing

The cup explains how this studio makes games with Claude. One step at a time. Click through.

STEP 1 OF 6 · WHO IS TALKING

I'm the cup. Here is how we make games.

We're Live Culture Games, a small studio in Escondido, California. The founder has the ideas. Claude has the hands. I have the yogurt.

The founder's own line: "AI codes the game and assists me as a disabled person."

STEP 2 OF 6 · THE LIMITS ARE REAL

The founder is disabled.

ADHD, autism and vision problems. Walls of text are hard. Holding twenty things in your head is hard. Small grey writing is hard. That's it, that's the list. No pity needed — it's just the terrain.

STEP 3 OF 6 · WHO WRITES THE CODE

The founder does not write code. Claude does.

Claude, by Anthropic, writes every line of the game. The founder never types code. Not one line. Peel my lid — it's true all the way down.

STEP 4 OF 6 · WHO BRINGS WHAT

Two seats. Nothing overlaps.

  • THE HUMANThe ideas, the taste, the design, and the final say.
  • CLAUDEThe hands: code, checking, remembering, keeping track.

The human says what and why. Claude does how. Neither one does the other one's job.

STEP 5 OF 6 · HOW WE BEAT THE LIMITS

Five habits. Each one earns its place.

  • 01Big readable pages instead of walls of text.
  • 02One thing at a time.
  • 03Claude remembers, so the founder doesn't have to hold it all.
  • 04The human plays every build and decides if it works before it ships.
  • 05The source — the real files — is the truth. Not anyone's memory.

This page is built the same way. Big type. One panel. You're looking at the method.
