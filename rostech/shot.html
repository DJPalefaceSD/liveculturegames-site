Shot

Hand Claude the picture on your clipboard in one command.

MEDIUM · ControlsON TRACKv0.1.0tested on Claude Code 2.1.287 on Windows
ON THE DASH
/shot
/shotsaves the clipboard picture and puts a message for Claude in your prompt box. Press Enter to send it.
/shot is the band lined up?puts your own question in with it.
L1Shot saves the picture as a PNG in your temp folder, then writes one message naming that file into your prompt box.

Claude opens the file itself. It uses Windows' own clipboard on Windows, wl-paste or xclip on Linux, and pngpaste on macOS if you have it installed. The picture stays on your machine.
